# THE FRUIT

> Book page (table of contents): 74–75 · PDF p. 74–75 (estimated from extracted text)

Extended description: The harvest and the nourishing gift, The Fruit is the reward of the bloom. It holds the memory of the cycle and the promise of continuity. It is the card of generosity, of just harvest, of shared sweetness. In the tarot, it represents the capacity to give without impoverishing oneself, to become nourishment for the world while preserving one's inner seed. It teaches conscious fertility, the pleasure of transmitting, the wisdom of natural rhythm: each fruit falls when it is ripe.
Light keywords: Abundance, maturity, harvest, transmission, fertility, sharing, quiet joy, gratitude, accomplishment.
Shadow: The overripe fruit or the loss of taste. In its shadow, Fruit becomes excess: possession, greed, consumption. The harvest becomes accumulation, and generosity turns into control. Or, conversely, the fruit is not picked in time: it rots, is forgotten, withers. This card warns against attachment to results and the inability to let go of what has already given. The Fruit teaches to savor without holding back, to pick the right moment.
Shadow keywords: Excess, gluttony, exhaustion, loss of flavor, attachment to the past, stagnation, waste.
THE FRUIT evokes accomplishment and generosity. The ripe fruit releasing its seeds represents the cycle closing and beginning again, success transformed into nourishment and a promise for the future. It symbolizes abundance, fertility, and the transmission of living heritage.
Integration paths: Celebrate what has borne fruit, then offer that fruit to the world. Learn to harvest without hoarding. Recognize the natural end of one cycle as the beginning of another. Savor the sweetness of the present. In a reading, The Fruit evokes concrete accomplishment, the maturity of a project or a relationship, or the need to share what has been cultivated.
Exercise / Meditation: Take a fruit in your hands. Observe its shape, its scent, its texture. Before tasting it, thank everything that made it possible: the rain, the sun, the earth, time. Savor it slowly, like a prayer of gratitude. The Fruit is the sweetness of maturity, the luminous substance of accomplished time. It teaches gratitude, the wisdom of transmission, and the beauty of sharing.
