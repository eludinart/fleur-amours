# THE DEEP SOURCE

> Book pages (table of contents): 112–113 · PDF p. 112–113 (estimated from extracted text)

Extended description: The origin of life and the memory of the heart. The Deep Source is the invisible beginning, the place from which all things spring. It is the memory of the world, the purity of origins, the matrix of true emotions. In the tarot, it symbolizes a return to self, a reconnection to intuition, inner healing through gentleness. It is the card of the soothed heart, of peace rediscovered in the depths. It teaches that clarity comes from depth: the deeper you dive, the purer the water becomes.
Light keywords: Origin, purity, memory, intuition, healing, true emotion, source of life, inner peace.
Shadow: Repression or drowning within. Under its shadow, the Source becomes a bottomless well. It is the card of emotional repression, of fleeing into the depths, or of stagnant melancholy. One can get lost by digging too deep without resurfacing. The shadow warns that introspection without returning to the surface becomes self-forgetfulness. One must drink, not drown. THE DEEP SOURCE symbolizes inner resources and hidden origins. The underground water rising through the earth's strata represents buried wisdom, the subconscious aquifers, and resilience that draws from the depths. It is the card of authenticity and returning to oneself.
Shadow keywords: Melancholy, stagnation, escape, emotional confusion, isolation, repressed pain, excessive introspection.
Integration paths: Welcoming one's emotions without being engulfed by them. Diving into one's depths to draw wisdom. Rediscovering the peace of the heart through silence and inner listening. Allowing Life to flow again without resistance. In a reading, The Deep Source signals a return to the essential, healing in progress, or a connection to the origin of emotional life.
Soul resonance: The Deep Source is the liquid heart of the world, the wisdom of primordial water. It teaches that all healing comes from a return to fluidity, that inner peace is a wellspring, not a conquest.
