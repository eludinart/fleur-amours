Quick Spreads How to Tame Your Garden: The 3 Levels of Practice This tarot is as dense as a forest. To avoid getting lost, we offer you the Progressive Filtering method, which is the recommended way to learn. The more you filter the deck, the more targeted and easy to interpret your spread becomes. Learning 1, 2, or 3-card spreads adapts to these three levels. The Principle of the Double Approach (Intuitive Freedom) If you are comfortable with reading Tarot and the subtleties of the system, you are free to ignore the filtering rule. Each of the spreads below can be performed by shuffling the entire deck of 65 cards.
