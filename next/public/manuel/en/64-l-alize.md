# THE TRADE WIND

> Book pages (table of contents): 132–133 · PDF p. 132–133 (estimated from extracted text)


Extended description: The wind of peace and natural guidance. The Trade Wind is the breath of gentleness. It is the wind that accompanies without rushing, that pushes the sails without tearing them. In the tarot, it symbolizes quiet guidance, trust in the natural movement of things. It is the card of inner journey, harmonious movement, peaceful freedom. It teaches us to listen to the right current, to move with Life rather than against it. The Trade Wind commands nothing: it accompanies everything.
Light keywords: Serenity, trust, harmony, guidance, fluidity, balance, journey, constancy, gentle breath, tranquil freedom.
Shadow: Drifting or lack of momentum. In its shadow, The Trade Wind becomes an absence of wind. It is the card of indecision, of letting go, or of directionless dispersion. Too much flexibility becomes apathy, too much trust becomes abandonment. The shadowed Trade Wind warns that moving with Life is not drifting. True gentleness requires conscious presence.
Shadow keywords: Inertia, hesitation, sluggishness, dependence, passivity, escape, inability to choose, loss of direction.
The TRADE WIND symbolizes gentle and constant movement. The harmonious swirls represent the breeze that caresses and nourishes, the regular flow that favors growth. It is the card of benevolent constancy, natural cycles, and peaceful accompaniment.
Integration paths: Rediscover the joy of effortless natural movement. Trust the currents of Life without losing one's intention. Take time to breathe before acting. Let yourself be guided by the simple pleasure of existing. In a reading, The Trade Wind evokes a period of appeasement, fluidity, inner journey, or inspired travel. It is an invitation to navigate with Life, in trust.
Exercise / Meditation: Close your eyes and imagine a warm wind on your face. Breathe slowly and feel this breath gently push you forward. Let your body move slightly, guided by this invisible current. Feel the peace of the right movement, the one that forces nothing.
