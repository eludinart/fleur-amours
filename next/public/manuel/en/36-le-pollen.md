# THE POLLEN

> Book pages (table of contents): 76–77 · PDF p. 76–77 (estimated from extracted text)

Extended description: The diffusion of life and the creative breath. The Pollen is the traveler of the living, the breath that carries the promise of fertility. It is the card of movement, of sharing, of the propagation of ideas and seeds. In the tarot, it symbolizes vibrational communication: that which, from oneself, touches the world without being seen. It teaches trust in invisible circulation, the generosity of the gesture that sows without expecting return. The Pollen reminds us that every word, every act, every breath can become fertile.
Light keywords: Diffusion, sharing, transmission, circulation, inspiration, fertility, lightness, creative breath, interconnection.
Shadow: Dispersion and loss of substance. In its shadow, The Pollen becomes scattering. Energy disperses, intentions dissolve in the wind. It is the card of the rootless word, of creativity that scatters, or of projects lacking grounding. In excess, it also evokes contamination: the imposed idea, the message spread without consciousness. The Pollen reminds us that the wind carries everything, but every wind must know its origin. THE POLLEN symbolizes transmission and fertilization. Pollen grains carried by the wind or pollinators represent the traveling genetic message, fertile chance, and union at a distance. This card speaks of the dissemination of ideas, providential encounters, and collaborative creativity.
Shadow keywords: Distraction, dispersion, carelessness, superficiality, influence, loss of direction, empty words.
Integration paths: Sow without attachment to the result. Cultivate conscious lightness: let go of what needs to circulate. Speak with clarity, act with intention. Trust the invisible currents that connect beings. In a reading, The Pollen evokes a period of expansion, communication, transmission of ideas or energy, but invites us not to scatter ourselves.
Soul resonance: The Pollen is the breath of sharing, the love that moves by the grace of the wind. It teaches that all creation must circulate to remain alive. Energy correspondences: Element: Air and Fire
Polarity: Diffuse Masculine (active, volatile, fertilizing)
Symbolic correspondences: Transmission, Travel, Spirit of the wind
In resonance: Air (The Trade Wind), Life (The Bee), Fire (The Solar Wind)
Root question: "What idea or message do I need to disseminate around me?"
Exercise / Meditation: Go outside on a windy day. Breathe deeply. Imagine your breath becoming golden pollen, light, luminous, carrying inspiration. Let the wind carry it away, trusting that it will find fertile ground.
