Setting a Clear Intention: Moving from "Madame Irma" to "What's happening within me?" The Fleur d'ÅmÔurs Tarot isn't here to tell you if you'll meet your soulmate, if your ex will come back, or if everything will magically resolve itself. It serves a different purpose: to see what's happening within you, to illuminate a dynamic, to clarify an inner path. The cards don't decide for you. They show you how you stand in a situation, what forces are at play, where things are stuck, and where growth is calling. How to formulate a good intention? A clear intention here is not: "Will I find someone?" "Will he/she come back?" These questions place you in a passive waiting position, as if the answer lies outside of you. With this tarot, we aim for questions like: "What is unfolding within me in this relationship?" "What part of myself needs to be seen in this situation?" "What inner path is opening for me right now?" "What stance helps me navigate this transition?" "What is this crisis transforming in my way of loving?" You shift from: "What will happen to me?" to "How do I encounter myself in what I'm experiencing?" A Few Simple Guidelines Before drawing cards, you can check: Does my question focus on me rather than the other person? Does it open a path of awareness rather than a yes/no answer? Does it help me see a dynamic, a posture, a lesson to be learned? If not, rephrase. For example: Instead of: "Will this project succeed?" "What is this project touching within me?" "What is the current dynamic of this project, and what inner stance would help me support it?" Instead of: "Will I finally be happy in love?" "What in my way of loving is asking to be recognized and transformed today?" You are not asking a question to receive a magical solution in life; you are setting an intention to receive a living answer in your way of being alive. The cards don't tell you what to do. They show you where you are, what is working within you, and how you can walk more consciously on your own path of ÅmÔurs.
