# THE MIST

> Book pages (table of contents): 116–117 · PDF p. 116–117 (estimated from extracted text)

Extended description: The fertile mystery and the wisdom of the blur. The Mist is water become breath. It softens outlines, invites intuition, and protects the secret of things in becoming. In the tarot, it embodies contemplation, mystery, and reconciliation with the unknown. It teaches us to accept not understanding everything, to walk in uncertainty with grace. It is the card of daydreaming, imagination, and light presence. In the Mist, everything blends without being lost: the world breathes between two worlds.
Light keywords: Mystery, inspiration, intuition, poetry, dream, gentleness, transition, calm, patience, contemplation.
Shadow keywords: Confusion or loss of bearings. In its shadow, the Mist becomes fog. One gets lost in it, falls asleep in it, loses clarity. It is the card of mental fog, illusions, or lack of discernment. Sensitivity becomes confusion, daydreaming becomes escape. The shadow of the Mist warns: one cannot live forever in the in-between. The veil protects, but must not become a prison.
Shadow: Confusion, loss of meaning, illusions, escape, apathy, emotional blur, disorientation, forgetting reality. THE MIST evokes mystery and the in-between. The vapors that blend sky and earth, erase outlines, and veil forms represent creative ambiguity, the space of dreams and necessary blur. This card speaks of intuition, transition, and that which escapes reason.
Integration paths: Accept the blur as a stage, not a dwelling. Find clarity through trust, not control. Listen to your dreams as messages, without absolutizing them. Let yourself be guided by gentleness, without losing your way. In a reading, The Mist indicates a moment of transition, introspection, or subtle perception, and invites you to slow down to let clarity return on its own.
Soul resonance: The Mist is the benevolent mystery of the living. It teaches the wisdom of uncertainty, the beauty of impermanence. Root question: "Can I accept not having the answer right away and moving forward in the blur?"
Exercise / Meditation: Close your eyes. Imagine a morning when mist covers the landscape. Breathe slowly, feel the tenderness of this diffused light. Observe how everything still exists, simply veiled. Learn to see without your eyes.
