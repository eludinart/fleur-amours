# THE GREAT PASSAGE

> Book pages (table of contents): 190–191 · PDF p. 190–190 (estimated from extracted text)

Extended description: This card evokes great transitions: death, of course, but also all metamorphoses that require leaving the old to be reborn anew. It speaks of detachment, confident surrender, and welcoming the void as a fertile matrix. In the Great Passage, there is nothing to fear; only the part that refuses to open resists. It is the sacred threshold where the human ceases to want to "understand" and consents to cross over. It teaches us that dying is an act of trust: allowing life to reclaim its original form within us. The Great Passage is an invitation to naked faith, to peace in the face of the inevitable, to tenderness towards what is fading. What disappears here is reborn elsewhere. What dies is never lost, only transformed. Liberation, transcendence, peace in letting go. Trust in the natural cycles of life and death. Acceptance of change as a path toward light.
Light keywords: Transmutation, Faith, Detachment, Passage, Renewal, Inner freedom, Sacred recycling.
Shadow keywords: Fear of death, refusal of change, attachment to the known. Inability to grieve, resistance to the flow of life.
Shadow: THE GREAT PASSAGE The Great Passage is the door between two worlds. It is not an end, but a slipping, a return to the essential movement of life. It represents the moment when form fades to become energy again, when consciousness returns its breath to the invisible.
Shadow keywords: Blockage, Denial, Refusal, Fear, Fixation, Closure.
Integration paths: Do not try to hold onto what is leaving. Thank what has accompanied you, then let go gently. Trust the intelligence of life: it never destroys, it transforms. Cross the threshold without struggle. What you believe you are losing, you are becoming again in another form.
Soul resonance: In a soul reading, the Great Passage is the experience of psychic Death-Rebirth. It represents the radical dissolution of an ego structure, a belief, or a social role that has become too rigid. The process is necessary to reach a higher level of consciousness integration. The message is: trust the chaos, it holds the seed of the most evolved form of yourself.
Energy correspondences: Elements: Fire / Ether, purification and dissolution.
Polarity: Masculine (active in the act of liberation).

Exercise / Meditation: Sit in stillness. Think of a situation, a relationship, a part of yourself that you feel is ready to end. Breathe deeply. Inhale gratitude, exhale release. Visualize a gentle flame before you: entrust it with what you are leaving behind. Watch the smoke rise, dissolve. Repeat inwardly: "I return to the fire what has lived. I enter the new breath."
