Love as a Prism of Consciousness It quickly became apparent to me that these eight forms were not mere fleeting sentiments. My proposition is that they constitute the eight fundamental tones of the Sap of the Universe, and that they can be seen as eight doors of perception or circuits of consciousness through which the human being experiences their reality. Romantic relationships, from this perspective, are the most powerful laboratory for revealing the state of these circuits.
