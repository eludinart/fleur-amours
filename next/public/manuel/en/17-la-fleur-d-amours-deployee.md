Open Deck or Closed Deck In the Deployed Fleur d’ÅmÔurs, the base cards are already laid out on the table: The Petals of the Fleur d'ÅmÔurs and the Cycle of the Plant serve as the structure. In this configuration, only the cards of the Elements, the Cycles of the Elements (Earth, Water, Air, Fire, Ether), and the Cycle of Life will be drawn or chosen. In a closed deck, you shuffle the decks of the Elements, their cycles, and the Cycle of Life, then draw the cards without looking at them beforehand. You let synchronicity decide which elemental qualities will illuminate each area of the Deployed Fleur d’ÅmÔurs. In an open deck, you spread out these same Element and elemental cycle cards and choose them consciously. You decide which cards to place on a particular root, stem, flower, or petal of the Fleur d'ÅmÔurs. The cards then become a vocabulary for articulating what you already perceive about your situation. There is no right or wrong way to do it. The closed deck emphasizes surprise and synchronicity; the open deck emphasizes choice, awareness, and expression. You can also combine the two, for example, drawing some cards blindly and choosing others while looking at them. 28 1. Activating the Card: Spreading the Garden To transform this static map into a living spread, you will now use the other card families. Shuffle together: the cards of the Elements and their cycles, the cards of the Cycle of Life. You obtain the decks of "moving forces." Set a global intention, for example: "What is the configuration of my Garden of ÅmÔurs right now?" or "What is unfolding deeply in my Path of Love today?" Draw 3 to 5 cards from these decks, one by one. Each card drawn is placed on the map where it feels right: on a stage of the Cycle of the Plant (where something is growing, blocked, fruiting, transforming), or on a petal of the Fleur d'ÅmÔurs (where a form of love is activating or needs attention), or in the center, on the Flower itself (when the card touches the entire garden). You can decide that: the first card shows the most sensitive point of the moment, the second what supports or complicates, the third (or more) what is being born, transforming, or wanting to be transmitted. 31 3. Reading the Map You can now read the spread on three levels: The Plant: At which stage of the cycle are the cards placed? Is everything happening mainly at the Root, the Flower, the Fruit, the Nectar...? This shows you where the work is happening in your growth process. The Forms of Love: Which petals are activated by the drawn cards? Is it primarily Agapè, Éros, Philia, Philautia...? You see which ways of loving are at the forefront right now. The Forces and Moments of Life (Elements + Life): What do these cards reveal about the climate (the elements) and the existential moment (cycle of life) you are going through? For example: a Fire card on Mania at the "Flower" stage, or an Earth card on Pragma at the "Root" stage, etc. You can conclude with a summary sentence, such as: "At this moment, my Garden of ÅmÔurs is unfolding mainly at the level of... The most active forms of love are... And the moving forces speak to me of..." 32 4. Usage This spread can serve as: -a snapshot of your current state, -a basis for tracking evolution (by repeating the spread at different times), -a support for longer work (writing, meditation, artistic expression, drawing your own Fleur d'ÅmÔurs based on what you have seen). The map remains the same, but each time, it is the cards that are placed upon it that tell the story of the moment. 33
