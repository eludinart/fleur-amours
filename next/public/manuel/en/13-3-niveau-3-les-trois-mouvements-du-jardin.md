# 3. Level 3: The Three Movements of the Garden

> Book pages (table of contents): 20–20 · PDF p. 2–2 (estimated from extracted text)

Level 3: The Three Movements of the Garden This level integrates all influences. Cards used: The entire deck (all 65 cards). Usage: For deep readings and systemic exploration. 3-card spread, The Three Movements of the Garden (The Ecosystem) Intention: A three-part spread to observe the dynamics of a process: where it comes from, where you are, and where it can evolve if you continue on this path. This is not a predictive spread, but a snapshot of movement. Setup: Shuffle ALL of the tarot. Formulate a clear intention, for example: "What is the dynamic of this relationship today?" Draw three cards and lay them out from left to right: Card 1 — Yesterday's season, Card 2 — Today's season, Card 3 — The season that is opening. Reading: For each card, you can let yourself be guided by these questions: Card 1: "What patterns, habits, or climates does it highlight?" Card 2: "What is playing out now, at the center of my garden?" Card 3: "What is seeking to be born, mature, or transform?" Then, connect the three together: What coherent narrative emerges between the three movements? You can conclude with a summary sentence: "I have come from... (card 1), I am at... (card 2), and I am invited to evolve towards... (card 3)." 18

Extended description:
This spread, "The Three Movements of the Garden," is designed for profound exploration and understanding of systemic dynamics within any situation, particularly relationships. It utilizes the entire Fleur d'ÅmÔurs deck to capture a holistic view. The intention is to trace the flow of a process through time: its origins, its present state, and its potential future trajectory. It emphasizes movement and evolution rather than prediction.

Light keywords:
Process, dynamics, evolution, past, present, future, systemic view, holistic, flow, transformation, growth, understanding, context, trajectory.

Shadow keywords:
Stagnation, cycles, inertia, resistance, unresolved issues, missed opportunities, predictable patterns, limited perspective, cyclical traps, lack of progress.

Shadow:
When this spread is used in its shadow aspect, it can reveal how past patterns (Card 1) are creating a stagnant or problematic present (Card 2), leading to a future that is merely a repetition of the same issues, or a future that is blocked from emerging due to unresolved energies. It highlights a lack of conscious evolution and a tendency to remain stuck in unproductive cycles.

Integration paths:
1. Acknowledge the "season of yesterday" (Card 1) not as a burden, but as the fertile ground from which the present has sprung.
2. Fully inhabit and understand the "season of today" (Card 2), recognizing its challenges and gifts without judgment.
3. Openly receive the potential of the "season that is opening" (Card 3), seeing it as an invitation to conscious co-creation.
4. Weave the narrative of the three cards into a story of continuous becoming, embracing the journey.

Soul resonance:
This spread resonates with the soul's journey through cycles of experience, learning, and growth. It speaks to the inherent wisdom of life's unfolding, encouraging us to see ourselves as active participants in our own evolution, moving from one season of the soul to the next with awareness and grace.

Energy correspondences:
The entire Fleur d'ÅmÔurs deck represents the totality of life's energies. The three positions can be seen as representing the flow of time (past, present, future) or the stages of a process (origin, manifestation, potential).

Root question:
"What is the underlying dynamic of this situation, from its roots to its potential unfolding?"

Exercise / Meditation:
After laying out the three cards and reflecting on their individual meanings and the narrative they form, take a moment to visualize yourself as a gardener. See your life or the situation in question as a garden. Card 1 represents the soil and the seeds planted in the past. Card 2 is the current state of growth, the weather, and the tending you are doing now. Card 3 is the harvest that is ripening or the new planting that is being prepared. Feel the interconnectedness of these seasons within your garden.

Element:
All Elements (Earth, Air, Fire, Water) are present as the entire deck is used.

Polarity:
All Polarities (Masculine/Feminine, Yang/Yin) are present.

Symbolic correspondences:
The Garden: Represents the self, a relationship, a project, or any living system.
The Three Movements: Symbolize the flow of time, causality, and potential.
Seasons: Represent distinct phases of development, influence, and energy.

In resonance:
When the cards drawn for each position feel like natural continuations or logical progressions of each other, creating a clear and coherent story of movement and change.
