# THE BREATH

> Book pages (table of contents): 126–127 · PDF p. 126–127 (estimated from extracted text)


Extended description: The vital principle and the breath of the world. The Breath is the first movement of the living. It animates, connects, transmits. In the tarot, it symbolizes the Life that circulates between all kingdoms, the breath that makes the heart of the world beat. It teaches the simplicity of returning to the breath, the wisdom of the natural rhythm: inhalation (reception), exhalation (giving). It is the card of subtle beginnings, of creative breath, of invisible communication. The Breath is the soul of movement.
Light keywords: Life, circulation, breath, communication, balance, subtle energy, harmony, movement, present moment awareness.
Shadow: The cut-off breath or the lost wind. Under its shadow, The Breath freezes. It is the card of lack of air, of severed connection, of breath blocked by fear or tension. It evokes stress, oppression, loss of natural rhythm, or disconnection from the body. The shadow of The Breath is that of imbalance, inhalation without exhalation (accumulation), or exhalation without inhalation (exhaustion). It invites us to rediscover life's proper tempo. THE BREATH Represents the universal life force. The prana that animates all things, visible in the wind, light, and the space between. This card evokes conscious breathing, the energy that unites all life, and the perpetual movement of existence.
Shadow keywords: Anxiety, rushing, fatigue, disconnection, imbalance, loss of bearings, lack of communication.
Integration paths: Return to conscious breathing to re-establish flow. Connect with Life through breath: each inhalation is a gift from the world. Release speech and emotions through air. Rediscover the balance between activity and rest. In a reading, The Breath announces vital renewal, a resurgence of energy, or the re-established circulation of communication or a relationship.
Exercise / Meditation: Close your eyes. Inhale slowly through your nose, imagining you are receiving the breath of the world. Exhale gently through your mouth, as if returning to the world what it has entrusted to you. Continue until your breath becomes silent prayer.
