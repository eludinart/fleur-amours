# THE STEM

> Book pages (table of contents): 66–67 · PDF p. 66–67 (estimated from extracted text)


Extended description: The impulse of conscious growth The Stem is the movement of life towards the light. It is the column that connects the visible and the invisible, the quiet strength that supports blossoming. In the tarot, The Stem invites us to grow with flexibility: to stand tall without stiffening, to let the sap flow freely. It symbolizes the verticality of being, the alignment between the depths of the earth and the clarity of the sky. It teaches trust in upward movement, faithfulness to one's inner direction. Light keywords: Growth, alignment, direction, support, coherence, circulation, vital energy, dynamic stability. Shadow: Rigidity or rupture When the Stem stiffens, life no longer flows. It is the ego standing like a dry trunk, or the will that wants to force nature to grow too quickly. Conversely, a stem that is too flexible bends to every wind and loses its axis. In its shadow, this card warns against the imbalance between anchoring and expansion: growth must remain organic, not imposed. The Stem reminds us that true strength is fluid. THE STEM Symbolizes the link and the supporting structure. The stem that unites roots and leaves represents the vital channel, the column that maintains and transports. This card evokes structural integrity, the ability to stand straight against the winds, and the function of intermediary between earth and sky. Shadow keywords: Rigidity, pride, exhaustion, loss of axis, dependence, fragility, energy dispersion. Integration paths: Rediscover your axis through breath and posture. Let the sap of life flow without trying to control it. Stand tall in your truth, with flexibility. Listen to the natural direction of your inner impulse. In a reading, The Stem indicates ongoing growth, a need for alignment, or stabilization necessary before opening to the light. Exercise / Meditation: Sit or stand, spine straight. Imagine a root plunging into the earth and a stem rising to the sky. Feel the vital flow rising and falling within you, connecting grounding and inspiration. You are the plant growing between two worlds. The Stem is the column of the living, the vibrant link between the underworld and the light. It teaches that growing is accepting the tension between earth and sky, between what nourishes and what calls.
