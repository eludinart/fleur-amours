The Four Gates of the Garden of ÅmÔurs Intention This reading is a true systemic diagnosis. It allows you to open four essential perspectives on a complex situation (relationship, project, existential question) by looking at it through the fundamental filters that govern living things. The Four Gates reveal not only what is at play but, above all, where your levers of action lie: Love, Process, Environment, and the Meaning of the Story. Setup: Preparing the Four Decks (65 cards) For this reading, you will divide the 65 cards of your tarot into four distinct decks, called the "Gates," which you will place on your reading space. This is the most crucial step to ensure the accuracy of the message. Draw a single card from each of the four decks, in the order of your choice. The four drawn cards form a line, a square, or a diamond on your reading mat. Synthesis (The Key to Meaning) The power of this reading lies in how the four cards, drawn from different contexts, tell a single story. The goal is to create a unique sentence that summarizes the current dynamic. To achieve this, do not simply juxtapose the meanings. Read the cards as stages of a journey, using the following order to unlock the meaning: 26 1. The Starting Point (Heart & Climate) Begin with the Gate of the Heart (D1), which expresses the deep engine (desire, fear, love resource), and the Gate of Climate (D3), which gives you the immediate energy of the environment (the context). Ask yourself: "What is the essential desire of my heart in the face of my environment's reality?" (Ex: My heart is called by Éros (D1), but the context is that of Clay (D3): My passion is invited to take a malleable, patient, and concrete form, rather than a rapid explosion.) 2. The Challenge of Action (Time & Climate) Then introduce the Gate of Time (D2). Time is often the blocking factor. Is the energy of the Gate of Climate (D3) appropriate for the Process phase (D2)? Ask yourself: "Is the action demanded by the Climate (D3) in harmony with my current season (D2)?" (Ex: I feel the force of Fire (D3) but I am in the Dormant Seed phase (D2): This Fire energy must be channeled to warm the seed, not to burn it. The action must be internal, not external.) 3. The Deep Meaning (History & Synthesis) Finish with the Gate of History (D4). This card crowns the reading by giving a higher meaning to the entire process. Ask yourself: "What is the learning that this experience should allow me to integrate into my personal or collective story?" (Ex: Whatever the tension between cards 1, 2, and 3, the goal is to lead me towards Transmission (D4): I am living this so that, one day, I can teach or share it.) Synthesis Sentence (The Completion) Once these three dialogues are established, bring the four elements together into a single narrative sentence to seal the interpretation. Example of a Coherent Narrative: "Self-love (Philautia – D1) must accept a slow phase of taking Root (D2) before blossoming, because the climate demands that I trust the Invisible (D3). This process leads to an inevitable Metamorphosis (D4)." The Four Gates reading thus offers you a complete strategic vision, transforming the chaos of a situation into a clear and meaningful path. 27

Extended description:
This reading is a true systemic diagnosis. It allows you to open four essential perspectives on a complex situation (relationship, project, existential question) by looking at it through the fundamental filters that govern living things. The Four Gates reveal not only what is at play but, above all, where your levers of action lie: Love, Process, Environment, and the Meaning of the Story.

Light keywords:
Diagnosis, systemic, perspectives, complex situation, fundamental filters, levers of action, Love, Process, Environment, Meaning.

Shadow keywords:
Oversimplification, confusion, lack of clarity, missed opportunities, misinterpretation, superficial analysis, feeling stuck, lack of direction.

Shadow:
The shadow of this spread lies in a superficial or fragmented interpretation. If the cards are not read in relation to each other, or if the synthesis is not properly formulated, the diagnosis can be misleading. One might focus too much on one aspect (e.g., the emotional engine) and neglect the others, leading to incomplete or even erroneous conclusions. The complexity of the situation might then feel overwhelming rather than illuminating.

Integration paths:
1. **Holistic Interpretation:** Ensure all four cards are considered in dialogue, not in isolation.
2. **Narrative Synthesis:** Focus on creating a coherent story that connects the four perspectives.
3. **Actionable Insights:** Identify clear levers of action based on the synthesis.
4. **Patience and Trust:** Embrace the process and trust the wisdom revealed.

Soul resonance:
The Soul resonates with the deep wisdom of interconnectedness, understanding that every aspect of life—emotions, time, environment, and destiny—is interwoven. It seeks to find meaning and purpose within the unfolding narrative of existence, recognizing its role in the grand tapestry of life.

Energy correspondences:
- **Gate of the Heart:** Emotional energy, connection, desire, vulnerability.
- **Gate of Time:** Temporal energy, patience, growth cycles, rhythm, flow.
- **Gate of Climate:** Environmental energy, context, external forces, influences, atmosphere.
- **Gate of History:** Karmic energy, destiny, lessons, legacy, purpose.

Root question:
"What is the essential dynamic at play in this situation, and where are my points of leverage for transformation?"

Exercise / Meditation:
**The Four Gates Visualization:**
1. Find a quiet space and shuffle each of the four decks (Heart, Time, Climate, History) separately, focusing on the essence of each Gate.
2. Draw one card from each deck.
3. Place the cards before you. Close your eyes and visualize each Gate as a portal.
4. **Gate of the Heart:** Feel the core emotion or desire represented by the card.
5. **Gate of Time:** Sense the rhythm or stage of development indicated.
6. **Gate of Climate:** Perceive the surrounding energies or context.
7. **Gate of History:** Glimpse the deeper meaning or lesson.
8. Gently open your eyes and observe how these four energies interact. Allow a narrative to emerge, connecting the cards into a story of transformation.

Element:
The spread itself is a synthesis of all elements, as each Gate draws from different elemental energies (Heart often relates to Fire/Water, Time to Water/Earth, Climate to Earth/Air, History to Ether/Fire).

Polarity:
This spread is balanced, encompassing both internal (Heart, Time) and external (Climate, History) factors, as well as masculine (action-oriented aspects of Climate/History) and feminine (receptive aspects of Heart/Time) energies.

Symbolic correspondences:
- **The Four Gates:** Represent the fundamental dimensions of any situation or life experience.
- **The Decks:** Symbolize distinct realms of influence and understanding.
- **The Cards:** Act as messengers from each realm, offering specific insights.
- **The Synthesis:** Represents the alchemical process of integrating diverse energies into a unified understanding.

In resonance:
When facing a complex situation that feels overwhelming or unclear, and seeking a comprehensive understanding that reveals both the underlying dynamics and actionable pathways forward. It is for those who wish to move beyond surface-level analysis to grasp the deeper currents shaping their experiences.
