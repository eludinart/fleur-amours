# THE WAVE

> Book pages (table of contents): 118–119 · PDF p. 118–119 (estimated from extracted text)


Extended description: The right movement and the power of the flow. The Wave is the beating heart of the ocean. It symbolizes vitality, emotion in motion, the freedom of flow. In the tarot, it teaches us to follow the natural rhythm of emotions: to rise, to unfold, to descend, to begin again. It is the courage to express what is alive, the dance of feeling without fear of intensity. The Wave reminds us that life is never static; it breathes in oscillations. Light keywords: Movement, freedom, intensity, emotional expression, adaptability, cycle, vitality, liberation. Shadow: The storm or resistance. In its shadow, The Wave becomes a storm. This is the card of losing emotional control, of being overwhelmed, of struggling against the flow. When you try to control the sea, you drown. The shadowed Wave warns: any repressed emotion eventually returns stronger. Conversely, excessive intensity can be exhausting. The challenge is to learn to surf, not to flee, not to dominate. Shadow keywords: Disorder, excess, outburst, resistance, anger, instability, fatigue, emotional overflow. THE WAVE Embodies emotional strength and cyclical movement. The powerful wave rising under the sun represents rising energy, the power of emotions, and their capacity to transform. It also symbolizes impermanence: what rises will eventually fall, everything is passage and movement. Integration paths: Accept the cyclical nature of emotions. Let the wave pass without opposing it. Transform movement into dance, not combat. Find grace in the ebb and flow. In a reading, The Wave announces a period of inner or relational movement, a call to express what was contained, or to welcome emotional change as a driving force in life. Exercise / Meditation: Lie down and synchronize your breath with that of an imaginary wave. Inhale: it rises. Exhale: it descends. Feel the movement within you, identical to that of the world. You are not separate from the sea: you are one of its breaths. The Wave is the gesture of the world breathing. It embodies emotional freedom, the courage to be in motion.
