THE METAMORPHOSIS
The Metamorphosis is the great passage of the living through itself. It represents the dissolution of one form so that another can emerge. It is the chrysalis, the dark night of the soul, the suspended space between the known and the possible. Nothing dies, everything transforms, but this truth is only understood by going through it. In metamorphosis, the being sheds what it believed itself to be.

Integration paths: Breathe in the in-between. Seek neither to hold on nor to provoke. Honor the symbolic death of what you no longer are. Recognize that light is born only through emptiness. Metamorphosis is not destruction: it is revelation.

Exercise / Meditation: Visualize your being enveloped in a chrysalis of light. With each inhale, surrender to it more deeply. With each exhale, the shell cracks slightly. When you feel peace settling in, imagine your wings unfolding. Do not look at them, feel them. They know where to go.

Soul resonance: The Metamorphosis is the card of the Alchemical Crisis. It often appears after the Great Passage and represents the active phase of cellular and psychic transformation. The work is to navigate the liminal state (the chaos between two forms) without seeking to return to the past. This transitional state can be painful, but it remains necessary to allow the emergence of a new structure of the Self.
