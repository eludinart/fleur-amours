Emergency Anchoring: The Tutor Rule

This deck connects you to the raw power of life. It's possible that a card, particularly in its Shadow aspect (e.g., Mania, The Volcano, The Great Passage), resonates so strongly that it causes shock or distress. In such cases, immediately apply the safety protocol: The Tutor Rule.

Phase 1: Recognition and Breathing
Set aside the difficult card, face up. Immediately stop analyzing its message. Close your eyes and take three deep belly breaths, grounding your feet to the floor.

Phase 3: Action and Integration
Let the Resource Card guide the interpretation. It is your lifeline. Example: If you draw The Volcano (Shadow: Explosion) and your Resource Card is Philautia (Self-love), the action is clear: "I must express my truth (Volcano), but only by taking an action that respects me (Philautia), not by destroying myself or the other in anger." The Resource Card, in its Light dimension, provides the gentlest path for integration. Never remain alone when facing a Shadow.
