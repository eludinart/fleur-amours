# THE ROOTS

> Book pages (table of contents): 64–65 · PDF p. 64–65 (estimated from extracted text)

Extended description: The "Roots" card is the memory of the beginning, the silent gesture of life that sinks in order to rise better. It is absolute trust in the earth, the fertile slowness of what is preparing in the shadows. In the tarot, The Roots invites us to return to the base, to rediscover inner security, to reconnect with what nourishes us deeply. It is the card of stability, of the link to origin, of the body and of presence. It teaches that all growth begins with an anchor, and that what hides nourishes what shines.
Light keywords: Anchoring, stability, origin, memory, foundation, connection to the earth, security, trust, fertile slowness.
Shadow keywords: Rigidity, fear of change, nostalgia, dependence, isolation, stagnation, fatigue of origins.
Shadow: The rooting that becomes attachment. When it freezes, the energy of the Roots becomes confinement. The fear of change, attachment to the past, or nostalgia for the "known ground" then prevent any evolution. One can cut oneself off from the sky by wanting to cling too much to the earth. This card, in its shadow, speaks of the excessive need for security, the difficulty of uprooting oneself to grow elsewhere. It calls for balancing stability and movement. THE ROOTS embody invisible anchoring and the subterranean quest. The root network exploring the darkness of the earth represents hidden foundations, connection to the nourishing substrate, and wisdom that seeks in depth. It symbolizes stability, subterranean intuition, and what holds us upright.
Integration paths: Return to one's body, to one's breath, to the earth beneath one's feet. Recognize one's roots without being confined by them. Heal the relationship with the past and lineage. Find inner security in the very movement of life. In a reading, The Roots evokes a return to the essential, the need to root oneself before undertaking or rising.
Soul resonance: The Roots is the fertile silence of the earth. It teaches the power of immobility, the wisdom of returning to origin. Root question: "On what does my current security rest: on myself or on the outside?"
Exercise / Meditation: Stand barefoot on the ground. Close your eyes and imagine roots extending beneath you. With each exhale, they go deeper; with each inhale, they bring the strength of the earth back to your heart. Feel the quiet stability rising from the center of the world.
